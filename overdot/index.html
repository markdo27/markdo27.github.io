Overdot

A display typeface built from overlapping dots. One set of drawings, drawn seven ways: circles, points, a soft grid, ink traps, diamonds, organic lobes and echoing arcs. Each look comes in nine styles, three weights across three widths, and every glyph stays editable.

The fonts are generated live in this page. Choose a look, set it in the tester, redraw a letter in the editor and download OpenType fonts of what you see.

Language Support

Languages whose letters are all in the character set: Western and Central European, Vietnamese, Indonesian, Malay and Swahili among the Latin-script ones, and the scripts drawn in their own packs. Support is derived from the drawings themselves, so a language appears here only when every letter it needs is drawn.
